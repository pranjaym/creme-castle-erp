How to read this

Ratings arrive over about three days. Unhappy customers rate within hours, happy ones take up to three days: at one day old roughly 88% of eventual 1★ are in but only 70% of 5★. So a fresh day always reads worse than it will finish. The badge in the header compares the day's rated % against the same weekday over the last four weeks, using only days old enough to have settled, and tells you whether to trust the percentages yet. Counts are real either way.

Rated % is a store manager metric, not just sample size. Zomato shows the customer's number while the order is live, so managers call and ask for a rating. Swiggy does not expose the number, which is why Zomato is the platform where the rating is winnable. Across 45 days rated % ranges from 5.2% to 13.4% between our own stores, and outlets that collect more ratings score better (correlation +0.61 with average rating, −0.59 with bad share). Two consequences: an outlet's average is not comparable to another's unless you look at rated % beside it, and the stores in the bottom half have room to lift their score by calling more, without changing a single recipe.

Bad is 1–3★, good is 4–5★. 3★ sits with bad deliberately: on this platform it is a soft complaint from someone who will not bother complaining twice.

Item attribution is by presence, not blame. An item is credited with a rating if it was in the order. On multi-item orders every item carries the rating, so an item's average is a co-occurrence signal — corroborate across outlets before acting.

Never put Zomato and Swiggy on one axis. Zomato ratings are partly solicited by manager calls; Swiggy's are unprompted because no number is shared. The two are measuring different things.

Escalation has two tiers. Act today is a physical contaminant, an illness report, or an expiry or allergen failure: about one every two days, rare enough to be worth interrupting someone for. Quality alert is spoilage and staleness, which runs closer to one a day and belongs with central production rather than in an emergency box. Both ignore star count.
